### Data Engineer | Cloud Data Platforms (Azure & AWS) | Data Analytics | Business Intelligence
---
## About Me
I am a Data Engineer focused on designing and building reliable, scalable data pipelines that power analytics and decision-making.
I specialize in cloud data engineering on **Microsoft Azure** and **AWS**, building ETL/ELT pipelines, data warehouses, and lakehouse architectures using tools like Databricks, Snowflake, and Airflow. I also bring a strong analytics background — developing dashboards, automated reporting, and machine learning models on top of the data infrastructure I build.
My goal is to help organizations turn raw, disconnected data into clean, trustworthy, and accessible data products that drive better decisions at scale.
---
## What I Do 
Data Pipeline Development (ETL/ELT)
Cloud Data Engineering (Azure, AWS)
Data Warehousing & Lakehouse Architecture (Databricks, Snowflake)
Workflow Orchestration (Airflow)
Dashboard Development ( Excel, Power BI)
Data Cleaning & Preparation
Business Problem Solving with Data
